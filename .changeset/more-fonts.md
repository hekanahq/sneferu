Font catalogue: 14 new families. Latin serif (Playfair Display, Cormorant Garamond, Fraunces), condensed display (Oswald, Bebas Neue), rounded (Nunito, Fredoka), modern sans (DM Sans, Manrope), Arabic serif (Amiri, Noto Naskh Arabic) and Arabic display (El Messiri, Reem Kufi, Lalezar). Serif families get a `serif` generic fallback. Hekana must host the new files before a theme uses them.
